# TDD – REQ-INV-105: Customer PO Reference and Delivery Instructions on sales invoices

## 1. Summary

**What you asked for and what this design delivers**

| Feature in the requirement (REQ-INV-105-FRD.docx) | This design |
|---|---|
| New Delivery Instructions field (up to 150 characters) on the sales order header | Builds it |
| Delivery Instructions shown on the sales order, Delivery tab, editable | Builds it |
| Delivery Instructions shown read-only on the posted sales invoice, General tab | Builds it |
| Delivery Instructions copied automatically from the order to the posted invoice | Builds it |
| Customer PO Reference (the order's **Customer reference**) on the printed invoice | Builds it (the field is already copied to the posted invoice by standard; only the printout is new) |
| Delivery Instructions on the printed invoice | Builds it |
| Printed sales invoice redesigned to look like Figure 1 (blue banner, boxed bill-to and invoice details, blue line header, totals block) | Builds it, as a new invoice report format |
| Standard invoice content not shown in Figure 1 (logo, bank details, delivery address, due date, tax breakdown, notes, footer texts, country blocks) | Left out, as you answered ("skip them") |

The design adds a Delivery Instructions field to sales orders and posted customer invoices and copies it when the invoice is posted. It also adds a new sales invoice report, `DYLO_SalesInvoice`, laid out like Figure 1. The report is registered in Print management and made the default format for the Customer invoice document. Everything is built with extensions and Chain of Command in the new model **DYLOExtensions**.

## 2. Scope and assumptions

In scope: sales order invoices (Print management document *Customer invoice*, `PrintMgmtDocumentType::SalesOrderInvoice`). Free text invoices, pro forma invoices printed from the order, confirmations and packing slips are not changed.

Assumptions (please check them; each one can still be corrected):

1. **Which order's Delivery Instructions go on a combined invoice.** You asked for "the first order". When several orders are invoiced together, the posted invoice takes the Delivery Instructions of the order the invoice is created under. That is the order whose number prints as *Sales Order* on the invoice; with summary invoicing it is the summary order picked by the *Summary update* setting in Accounts receivable parameters. The other orders' instructions are not printed.
2. Delivery Instructions can be edited whenever the sales order header can be edited. Nothing new locks the field, and it is not copied back to the order or changed after posting.
3. **Whole redesign.** The whole printed invoice is made to look like Figure 1, as you answered. It is a new report format. The standard invoice report stays available in Print management, unchanged.
4. **What in Figure 1 is not printed:**
   - The red dashed box, its pink background, the "NEW HEADER FIELDS" call-out and the arrow are annotation. The two new fields print in the details panel as drawn, with labels in the same grey as the other labels, with no red border and no pink background.
   - The caption under the page is not printed.
   - "Contoso Corp.", the customer, the items and the amounts are sample data.
   - The word "Preview" is not printed. The line under the title prints **Original** or **Copy**, as the standard invoice does.
5. **The banner** (the blue box, top left) prints your company's name from the legal entity in white. As you answered, no company logo is printed.
6. **"BILL TO / SHIP TO"** prints the customer account, the invoice name and the **invoice address**. The delivery address is not printed, as you answered.
7. **Title.** An invoice prints **SALES INVOICE**. A credit note prints the standard document title in capitals (for example CREDIT NOTE), so a credit note is never titled "Sales invoice".
8. **Payment Terms** prints the standard payment-condition text the invoice already uses: the payment terms description. If the invoice has a fixed due date, or the legal entity is in Spain, it is the due date instead (standard behaviour).
9. **Totals block:**
   - *Subtotal* = invoice amount less sales tax, so the three lines always add up. Any charges and end discount are included in it.
   - *Sales Tax* is the total sales tax, with no percentage. An invoice can carry several tax rates, so "(8.25%)" in the picture is not printed.
   - *Total Invoice Amount* is followed by the invoice currency code (for example "5,358.38 USD").
   - Line amounts print in the currency's number format without a "$" sign.
10. Amount columns are **left-aligned, as drawn in Figure 1**.
11. The new report has one layout for all countries. The country-specific invoice designs (Czech, Hungarian, Polish, Thai, Indian and so on) are not copied into it. The legal texts and registration numbers they carry are not printed in the new layout, as you answered. A legal entity that is legally required to print them should keep the standard format in Print management.
12. On invoices that run to several pages, the line table continues on the next pages with its blue header repeated. The banner, panels and totals print once: the header block on page 1 and the totals after the last line. No page footer is printed because Figure 1 shows none.
13. Measurements in the layout specification (section 4.6) are taken from Figure 1 by proportion. They may be adjusted by a few points to fit.

Not in scope: data entities, import/export of the new field, Retail/Call center order screens, workflows.

## 3. Functional design

**Sales order (Sales and marketing > Sales orders > All sales orders)**

- The header view, **Delivery** tab, gets a new field **Delivery instructions** (free text, 150 characters). Sales coordinators enter or change it before invoicing.

**Posting a sales invoice**

- Posting copies Delivery instructions from the sales order header to the posted invoice header (customer invoice journal). Users do not enter it again.

**Posted invoice (Accounts receivable > Inquiries and reports > Invoices > Invoice journal)**

- The **General** tab of the header shows **Delivery instructions**, read-only, next to the invoice number and invoice date.

**Printed sales invoice**

- A new report format, **DYLO_SalesInvoice.Report**, prints the invoice laid out like Figure 1:
  - a blue banner with the company name, "SALES INVOICE" and Original/Copy at the top right, and the company name and address;
  - a grey rule;
  - the BILL TO panel on the left;
  - the invoice details panel on the right: Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Sales Order, Payment Terms, **Customer PO Reference** (the order's Customer reference) and **Delivery Instructions**;
  - the line table with a blue header and shaded alternate rows;
  - the Subtotal / Sales Tax / Total block.
- It becomes the default format for the Customer invoice document. Where Print management setup already names a format, it must be chosen there (section 5). Preview, print, e-mail and reprint all use it.

## 4. Technical design

All objects are in the new model **DYLOExtensions** (USR layer). New objects use the prefix `DYLO_`, and extensions are named `<Standard>.DYLOExtensions` / `<Standard>_DYLO_Extension`.

The model descriptor references `ApplicationPlatform`, `ApplicationFoundation`, `ApplicationSuite` and `Directory`.

### 4.1 Data model

| Object | Change |
|---|---|
| EDT `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions` (new, String) | StringSize 150. Label `@DYLOExtensions:DeliveryInstructions` "Delivery instructions". HelpText `@DYLOExtensions:DeliveryInstructionsHelp` "Shipping notes for the customer's receiving location, printed on the invoice." |
| `SalesTable.DYLOExtensions` (table extension) | New field `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions` (EDT `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions`). |
| `CustInvoiceJour.DYLOExtensions` (table extension) | New field `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions` (EDT `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions`), AllowEdit = No. |
| `SalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp.DYLOExtensions` (table extension) | New field `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions` (EDT `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions`). This is the report's header temp table. |

Customer PO Reference needs no new field. `CustInvoiceJour.CustomerRef` is copied from `SalesTable.CustomerRef` by standard posting. `SalesInvoiceDP.populateSalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp` sets `SalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp.CustomerRef` for every country (line `salesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp.CustomerRef = _custInvoiceJour.CustomerRef;`), and the field is already in the report dataset `SalesInvoiceHeaderFooterDS`.

### 4.2 Business logic – copy at posting

**`CustInvoiceJour_DYLO_Extension`** (new class, `[ExtensionOf(tableStr(CustInvoiceJour))]`, final)

- Wraps the public method `initFromSalesTable(SalesTable salesTable, SalesParmTable _salesParmTable = null)`. `SalesInvoiceJournalCreate.initInvoiceHeaderFromSourceTable()` calls it with the order the invoice header is created from (`salesParmTable.salesTable()`).
- Code: `next initFromSalesTable(salesTable, _salesParmTable); this.DYLO_DeliveryInstructions = salesTable.DYLO_DeliveryInstructions;`
- It runs before the journal header is inserted, so no extra update is made.

The method is public, not final and has no `Hookable(false)`/`Wrappable(false)` attribute, so it can be wrapped from another model. Its parameter name `salesTable` must be kept as declared.

### 4.3 Business logic – report data provider

**`SalesInvoiceDP_DYLO_Extension`** (new class, `[ExtensionOf(classStr(SalesInvoiceDP))]`, final)

- Wraps `protected void populateSalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp(CustInvoiceJour _custInvoiceJour, CompanyInfo _companyInfo)`.
- After `next`, it sets `salesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp.DYLO_DeliveryInstructions = _custInvoiceJour.DYLO_DeliveryInstructions;`. The buffer is a protected class variable, so it can be reached from the extension. The record is inserted afterwards by `insertIntoSalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp`.

What the data provider fills, checked in the 10.0.49 code:

| Field used by the layout | Filled? |
|---|---|
| `SalesInvoiceHeaderFooterTmp.CompanyName`, `CompanyAddress` | Filled for every country, except on a blind shipment (`ShipCarrierBlindShipment`), where they stay empty by standard. They are then blank on the invoice. |
| `InvoicingName`, `InvoicingAddress`, `InvoiceAccount`, `SalesId`, `CustomerRef`, `PaymentCondition`, `InvoiceDate`, `IsInvoiceCreditNote` | Filled for every country. |
| `SalesInvoiceTmp.InvoiceId`, `ItemId`, `Name`, `Qty`, `SalesUnitTxt`, `SalesPrice`, `LineAmount`, `InvoiceAmount`, `SumTax`, `CurrencyCode` | Filled in `populateSalesInvoiceTmp` / `insertInvoiceHeaderInformation` for every country. |
| Delivery instructions | New: filled by the extension above. |

### 4.5 Report and print management (skills d365-print-management-report, d365-report-layout)

**`DYLO_SalesInvoice`** (new report, copy of the standard `SalesInvoice`)

- Copied with `.dylo/copy-standard.ps1`. The datasets, the data provider `SalesInvoiceDP`, the contract and every report parameter are kept as they are.
- Changes to the datasets:
  - In `SalesInvoiceHeaderFooterDS`, add the fields `DYLO_DeliveryInstructions` and `IsInvoiceCreditNote`, in the AxReport dataset **and** in the design's RDL `<Fields>`.
  - Restore the dataset fields from the data provider after the table extension is built.
- Designs:
  - Keep **one** design, `Report`, and remove the country designs (`ReportEE`, `ReportLT`, `ReportHU`, `ReportLV`, `ReportCZ`, `ReportPL` and the others in the copy).
  - The `Report` design's body, page header and page footer are replaced by the layout in 4.6. Its parameters and datasets stay.
- The report runs through the standard `SalesInvoiceController` from Print management, so no controller or menu item is added.

**`DYLO_PrintMgmtReportFormatPopulator_Extension`** (new class, `[ExtensionOf(classStr(PrintMgmtReportFormatPopulator))]`, final)

- Wraps the protected method `addDocuments()`. After `next`, it calls `this.addOther(PrintMgmtDocumentType::SalesOrderInvoice, ssrsReportStr(DYLO_SalesInvoice, Report), ssrsReportStr(DYLO_SalesInvoice, Report), #NoCountryRegionId);` (with the `#PrintMgmtSetup` macro).
- Without it the format does not appear in Print management setup.

**`DYLO_PrintMgmtDocType_EventHandler`** (new class)

- Subscribes to `PrintMgmtDocType.getDefaultReportFormatDelegate` (ApplicationFoundation). For `PrintMgmtDocumentType::SalesOrderInvoice` it sets `_result.result(ssrsReportStr(DYLO_SalesInvoice, Report))`, so the new report **becomes the default**.
- The client has no other code, so there is no competing subscriber.

## 5. Setup and data

1. After deployment, open **Accounts receivable > Setup > Forms > Form setup > Print management**. Opening the form registers the new format.
2. Under **Customer invoice > Original** (and **Copy**), choose report format **DYLO_SalesInvoice.Report**. Do the same on any customer- or account-level Print management overrides. Where nothing was saved before, the new report is already the default.
3. No data migration. Delivery instructions are empty on existing orders and on invoices posted before go-live.
