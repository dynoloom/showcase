A daily batch job looks at every open customer invoice in the company and works out how many days it is
past due. When the invoice has just reached one of the overdue tiers that finance set up (for example 15,
30, 60 days), the job emails the finance or collections team. Every email attempt, sent or failed, is
written to a new log. The log is shown on a new screen **Credit and collections > Inquiries and reports >
Overdue invoice notification log**. On that screen users see the email that went out and any delivery
error. They can also resend a notification, purge old log records and start the batch job.

## 2. Scope and assumptions

In scope: the batch job, the overdue-tier setup, the notification log table and screen, resend, purge,
menu entries, security and labels.

Assumptions (correct us if any is wrong):

1. **Which invoices count.** An invoice is a posted customer invoice journal (`CustInvoiceJour`): sales order
   invoices and free text invoices. Project invoices and invoices entered through a general journal are
   not in `CustInvoiceJour` and are **not** included. Credit notes are not included.
2. **Open amount and due date.** The open amount and the due date come from the customer's open
   transactions (`CustTransOpen`). So partly paid invoices are included. For invoices with a payment
   schedule, the earliest installment that is still open is used. Days overdue = run date − that due date.
3. **Grace period.** An invoice is only considered when its due date is strictly earlier than run date − Grace
   period days. Grace period defaults to 0.
4. **Tiers.** The batch sends an alert only when an invoice has reached a tier (days overdue ≥ tier
   days) and no **successful** alert was sent for that invoice and tier. Invoices that are overdue but
   below the first tier get no alert (your answer). If the job did not run for some days and an invoice
   has passed two tiers at once, only the **highest tier reached** is sent. The lower tier is skipped.
5. **One log line per invoice and tier.** If sending fails, the log line is marked Failed with the error.
   The next batch run (or a manual Resend) tries again **on the same log line**. It updates the status,
   the error, the last attempt time and the attempt count, so the log does not fill up with a new failed
   line every day.
6. **Resend** sends the subject, body and recipient that are stored on the log line. That is the email as it was
   built, with the amounts from that time. Resend is allowed for any selected line. For a line that is
   already Sent, the user is asked to confirm first.
7. **Purge** deletes log lines created more than 180 days ago. The 180 days are a field on the setup form,
   with a default of 180.
8. **Customer group filter** on the batch job takes one customer group. Empty means all customers. If you want
   different recipients per group (the mockup shows finance@ and collections@), schedule one batch job
   per group, each with its own recipient.
9. **Company.** The batch job works in the company it is scheduled in. Each legal entity that needs alerts
   gets its own scheduled job and its own setup.
10. **Email delivery** uses the email provider configured in **System administration > Setup > Email
    parameters** (SMTP or Microsoft Graph, non-interactive). The email is sent at once, not through the email
    distributor queue, so that the log can record Sent / Failed and the error text immediately. The email is plain text.
11. **Sender address** is a field on the setup form. If it is empty, the default sender from the email parameters is
    used.
12. **Security.** This design delivers a new role "Overdue invoice notification manager" with two duties. Your
    administrator assigns the role, or adds the duties to your collections roles in Security configuration.
13. **Screen look (as you agreed).** The log screen follows the standard D365 "Simple list and details" pattern:
    grid on the left, details on the right. The top navigation bar, company badge and breadcrumb in the mockup
    are the standard D365 frame and are not built. The green/red status cells and the red "days overdue"
    figures are built. The right pane's dark blue header band, the pink error box and the typewriter-style font
    of the email preview are not built. They show as a standard group caption and read-only text boxes.
14. **No "View Email Payload" button (as you agreed).** The right-hand pane shows the recipient, subject and full
    body of the email as sent. There is no separate pop-up of the raw message.

## 3. Functional design

### 3.1 Setup – Credit and collections > Setup > Overdue invoice notification setup
- **General** tab: Sender email address; Log retention days (default 180).
- **Overdue tiers** tab: a grid of tiers with Days overdue (integer, > 0, unique), Description and Active. Finance users
  add, change or deactivate tiers themselves.

### 3.2 Batch job – Credit and collections > Periodic processes > Send overdue invoice notifications
Dialog parameters: Grace period days (default 0), Customer group (optional), Recipient email address
(required). It runs in batch with the standard Batch processing / Recurrence options (for example daily at 06:00).

Each run:
1. Reads the active tiers. If there are none, or the recipient is empty, it stops with an error.
2. Selects every open invoice as in section 2 (assumptions 1–3 and 8).
3. For each invoice, finds the highest active tier whose days ≤ days overdue.
4. If a **Sent** log line exists for this invoice and tier, it skips the invoice. If a **Failed** line exists, it
   retries on that line. Otherwise it builds the email and creates a new log line.
5. Sends the email and records Sent or Failed with the error text.
6. At the end, writes an infolog summary: invoices checked, sent, failed, skipped.

Email (from labels, so it can be translated):

```
Subject: [OVERDUE ALERT] Invoice <InvoiceId> - <DaysOverdue> Days Past Due
Body:
Dear Finance Team,

Invoice <InvoiceId> for customer <CustAccount> (<CustomerName>)
original amount <CurrencyCode> <InvoiceAmount>, outstanding amount <CurrencyCode> <RemainingAmount>,
is now <DaysOverdue> days past due.

Invoice date: <InvoiceDate>
Original due date: <DueDate>
Overdue tier: <TierDays> days
Action Required: Please initiate collections follow-up.
```
Dates and amounts are formatted with the batch user's settings (`date2StrUsr`, `num2Str` with 2 decimals).

### 3.3 Notification log – Credit and collections > Inquiries and reports > Overdue invoice notification log
- Grid (left), newest first: Invoice ID, Customer, Due date, Days overdue, Status, Notification sent to. The Status cell
  is green for Sent and red for Failed. Days overdue is shown in red.
- Filter area above: standard quick filter (Invoice ID / Customer), a Status filter (All / Sent / Failed) and a
  read-only line "All (n) | Sent (n) | Failed (n)".
- Right pane "Notification & error details": Delivery error (shown only when there is one), then To, Subject and Body
  of the email as sent, plus Invoice date, Customer name, Original amount, Outstanding amount, Currency, Tier, Attempts,
  Last attempt and Sent at.
- Action pane: **Resend notification** (on the selected lines), **Run batch now** (opens the batch dialog), **Purge
  old logs** (asks for confirmation, then deletes lines older than the retention days). No "View Email Payload" button.

### 4.2 Data model

**Enum `DYLO_OverdueNotificationStatus`**: Sent (0), Failed (1). Labels "Sent", "Failed".

**Table `DYLO_OverdueNotificationTier`** (TableGroup Group, SaveDataPerCompany Yes)
| Field | EDT / type | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Description | `Description` | |

Static `highestReachedTier(int _daysOverdue)`: `select firstonly TierDays from tier where tier.Active && tier.TierDays <= _daysOverdue order by TierDays desc`; returns 0 when none.

No delete action: the log is kept when invoices are removed by standard clean-up jobs.

### 4.3 Business logic

No standard method is wrapped and no standard data is changed. The logic only reads `CustInvoiceJour`,
`CustTrans`, `CustTransOpen` and `CustTable`, and calls the public `SysMailerFactory` / `SysMailerMessageBuilder` APIs.

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationContract`** (`[DataContractAttribute]`, implements `SysOperationValidatable`)
| Parameter | Type | Required | Single / multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| GracePeriodDays | `Days` (int), default 0 | yes (≥ 0) | single |
| CustGroupId | `CustGroupId` | no (empty = all) | single |
| RecipientEmail | `Email` | yes | single (one address or a distribution list) |

`validate()`: recipient not empty and a valid address (construct `System.Net.Mail.MailAddress` in try/catch,
error label on failure); grace ≥ 0.

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationController`** extends `SysOperationServiceController`.
`construct()` → `new(classStr(DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationService), methodStr(DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationService, process), SysOperationExecutionMode::Synchronous)`;
`defaultCaption()` returns label "Send overdue invoice notifications"; `static main(Args)` → `construct().startOperation()`.
Batch allowed (`canGoBatch` returns true).

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationService`** extends `SysOperationServiceBase` – `process(DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationContract _c)`:
1. `runDate = DateTimeUtil::getSystemDate(DateTimeUtil::getCompanyTimeZone())`; `cutoff = runDate - GracePeriodDays`.
2. Stop with error if there is no active `DYLO_OverdueNotificationTier`.
3. Selection (read-only):
   ```
   while select custTransOpen
       group by custTrans.RecId
       ... minof(DueDate) of open lines where custTransOpen.DueDate < cutoff
       join custTrans where custTrans.RecId == custTransOpen.RefRecId && custTrans.AmountCur > 0
       join custInvoiceJour where custInvoiceJour.InvoiceId == custTrans.Invoice
             && custInvoiceJour.InvoiceAccount == custTrans.AccountNum
             && custInvoiceJour.InvoiceDate == custTrans.TransDate
             && custInvoiceJour.LedgerVoucher == custTrans.Voucher
       [exists join custTable where custTable.AccountNum == custInvoiceJour.InvoiceAccount && custTable.CustGroup == CustGroupId]  // only when the filter is given
   ```
   Result per invoice: the earliest due date of its open lines past the cutoff. RemainingAmount is a second
   `select sum(AmountCur)` on all `CustTransOpen` lines of the same `CustTrans` (all open installments, not only past due).
4. `daysOverdue = runDate - minDueDate`; `tier = DYLO_OverdueNotificationTier::highestReachedTier(daysOverdue)`; 0 → skip.
5. `log = DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationLog::findByInvoiceTier(jour.RecId, tier, true)`: Sent → skip; Failed → retry on it;
   none → init a new line from the invoice, build subject/body with `DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationEmail::buildSubject/buildBody`, insert.
6. `DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationEmail::sendAndLog(log)`.

Each invoice runs in its own `ttsbegin/ttscommit` inside try/catch, so one failure never stops the run. Infolog summary at the end.

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationEmail`** (shared by batch and resend)
- `buildSubject(log)`, `buildBody(log)` – labels with `strFmt`.
- `sendAndLog(DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationLog _log)` (log selected for update):
  - `SysMailerMessageBuilder b = new SysMailerMessageBuilder(); b.addTo(_log.RecipientEmail).setSubject(_log.EmailSubject).setBody(_log.EmailBody, false);`
    plus `setFrom(params.SenderEmail)` when filled.
  - Remember `infologLine()`; call `SysMailerFactory::sendNonInteractive(b.getMessage())` inside try/catch (`Exception::CLRError`, `Exception::Error`).
  - Success: Status = Sent, ErrorText = '', SentDateTime = now. Failure (returns false or throws): Status = Failed,
    ErrorText = the infolog lines added since the remembered line (`infolog.text(i)`) plus the inner message of
    `CLRInterop::getLastException()` for a CLR error; when that is empty, the label "The email provider did not accept the message."
  - Always AttemptCount++, LastAttemptDateTime = now; `update()`.

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationResend`** (runs from an action menu item, `static main(Args)`): takes the selected
records from the caller data source (`MultiSelectionHelper`). If any selected line is Sent, it asks `Box::yesNo` first.
For each line it calls `sendAndLog` in its own transaction, shows info "n sent, n failed" and refreshes the caller
data source (`research(true)`) and the counts line (form method `updateCounts()`).

**`DYLO_OverdueInvoiceNotificationPurge`** (`static main(Args)`): asks `Box::yesNo` showing the cut-off date, then
`delete_from log where log.CreatedDateTime < DateTimeUtil::addDays(DateTimeUtil::utcNow(), -params.LogRetentionDays)`.
Shows info with the count and refreshes the caller.

## 5. Setup and data
1. System administration > Setup > Email parameters: make sure the non-interactive email provider (SMTP or
   Microsoft Graph) is configured and can send to the recipient address.
2. Credit and collections > Setup > Overdue invoice notification setup: enter the sender email address (optional),
   check the retention of 180 days and create the tiers (for example 15, 30, 60 days, Active).
3. Credit and collections > Periodic processes > Send overdue invoice notifications: enter Grace period, Customer
   group (optional) and Recipient; Batch processing = Yes; Recurrence daily, start 06:00. One job per legal entity (and per
   customer group if the recipients differ).
4. Assign the role "Overdue invoice notification manager" (or its duties) to finance and credit control users.
