## 1. Summary

**What you asked for and what this design delivers**

| Feature named in the requirement | This design |
|---|---|
| Lock direct editing of Credit limit on the customer | **Built.** Read-only for everyone except holders of a new "Finance Director" credit-limit privilege; read-only for everyone when a customer is created. |
| Credit limit increase request form (Request ID, customer, current limit, requested limit, net change, justification, requested by, approval status) | **Built.** New form under Accounts receivable > Customers > Credit limit increase requests. |
| Submission through the workflow action bar, with validation, and record locked while in review | **Built.** |
| Conditional routing: AR Team Lead only, or AR Team Lead then Finance Director, by the $25,000 / $100,000 thresholds | **Built.** The thresholds are a setting per legal entity (your answer); the routing itself is configured in the workflow editor. |
| Automatic update of the customer's credit limit on final approval, with approval date and final approver | **Built.** |
| Rejection, Request change | **Built** (standard workflow outcomes, handled by the new workflow). |
| Escalation of unactioned work items after 48 hours (24 h for the AR Team Lead in the diagram) | **Covered by standard workflow functionality**, as you asked: time limits and escalation paths are set per step in the workflow editor (section 5). Nothing is coded for it. |
| Credit limit decreases (from your answers) | **Built** through the same request form and workflow; the workflow approves them automatically. |

A sales coordinator raises a request for a customer. The current credit limit is filled in, they type the new limit and a justification, and submit it to workflow. On submit the system works out which approval path applies, using the two thresholds set per legal entity: a decrease, a single-step increase (AR Team Lead), or a two-step increase (AR Team Lead, then Finance Director). The workflow, set up in the standard workflow editor, routes the request on that basis. When the workflow approves it, the customer's credit limit is updated straight away, and the approval date and final approver are stamped on the request. A rejection leaves the customer unchanged.

Note: two standard features touch the same field. **Credit management > Credit limit adjustments** has its own approval workflow, and **customer change proposals** (Customer approvals) can also apply to Credit limit. This design does not use or change either of them (see the assumptions in section 2).

## 2. Scope and assumptions

**In scope:** a new request table and form, a new workflow type with one approval element, a number sequence for Request ID, two threshold parameters per legal entity, locking of the Credit limit field on the customer, security, and labels.

**Assumptions (correct them if they are wrong):**
1. **Decreases go through the request form too.** Your answer overrides AC-01: the form accepts any requested limit that *differs* from the current one. It blocks a request where the limit is unchanged, and one with a blank justification. A decrease still needs a justification.
2. **The thresholds are amounts in each legal entity's accounting currency.** That is the currency of the customer's credit limit. They are set in Accounts receivable parameters and default to 0 until you set them (see section 5).
3. **The approval path is fixed when the request is submitted.** It is recalculated on each resubmission. The path is:
   - *Decrease*: the requested limit is below the current limit.
   - *Two-step*: the net increase is above the increase threshold, **or** the requested limit is above the new-limit threshold.
   - *Single-step*: any other increase.
4. **"Current credit limit" is refreshed from the customer at submission.** On final approval the customer's limit is set to the requested limit, even if the limit changed in between (for example through a Finance Director edit); an info message is logged in that case.
5. **Request change** returns the request to *Draft*, where the submitter can edit and resubmit it. **Rejected** and **Canceled** are final: a new request must be raised.
6. **Only one open request per customer.** An open request is one in Draft, Submitted or Pending approval.
7. **Approval date and final approver are stored on the request**, not on the customer (the customer table has no such fields). On an automatically approved decrease, the final approver recorded is the workflow execution account.
8. **Who may still edit Credit limit directly:** users with the new privilege *Edit customer credit limit*, which is granted through the new role *DYLO Finance Director*. System administrators hold every privilege, so they can edit it too. When a customer is **created**, the field is read-only for everyone, including the Finance Director, and a new customer is saved with credit limit 0.
9. **The lock applies to the Customers form and to any save that runs the customer's validation**, which includes the data entities, Excel and data import.
   - The standard **Credit management credit limit adjustment** posting writes the limit directly, so the lock does not stop it. It stays governed by its own security and its own workflow.
   - Standard **customer change proposals** are assumed *not* to be enabled for Credit limit.
10. **Approvers are assigned by security role in the workflow editor.** The standard application has no "AR Team Lead" or "Finance Director" role, so this design adds the roles DYLO AR Team Lead and DYLO Finance Director. Users get them in addition to their existing roles.
11. **The workflow category is `CustCategory`** (Accounts receivable). The FRD names "CustWorkflowCategory", which does not exist in D365.
12. **Figure 1 is a picture of a screen, not of a printed document.** The form follows the standard *Simple list and details* pattern with the fields grouped as in Figure 1. The yellow bar and the Workflow button in the picture are the standard workflow message bar and action button, and the flow diagram is described in the workflow setup (section 5). No report is involved.

**Out of scope:** printing, notifications beyond standard workflow notifications, changes to Credit management.

## 3. Functional design

### 3.1 Customer master – Credit limit locked
- On *Accounts receivable > Customers > All customers*, the **Credit limit** field (Credit and collections FastTab) is read-only:
  - always, for users without the *Edit customer credit limit* privilege;
  - for everyone while a new customer is being created (record not yet saved).
- If anyone without the privilege changes the credit limit by another route (data entity, Open in Excel), the save fails with "Credit limit can only be changed through a credit limit request." Creating a customer with a credit limit other than 0 also fails, with "A new customer is created with credit limit 0; use a credit limit request to set it."

### 3.2 Credit limit increase requests (new form)
Menu: **Accounts receivable > Customers > Credit limit increase requests**. It is a list of requests on the left and the details on the right, laid out as in Figure 1:

| Left column | Right column |
|---|---|
| Request ID (number sequence, read-only) | Requested new limit (editable in Draft) and, next to it, Net change (read-only, calculated) |
| Customer account and customer name (account editable in Draft, name read-only) | Approval status (read-only) |
| Current credit limit (read-only, taken from the customer) | Business justification (mandatory, multi-line) |

The **Workflow** group (lower part of the details) shows Requested by, Approval path, Approval date and Final approver, all read-only.

The standard workflow message bar and **Workflow** button (Submit, Recall, Approve, Reject, Request change, Delegate, View history) appear at the top of the form.

**Status flow:**

| Event | Status |
|---|---|
| Record created | Draft |
| Submitted | Submitted |
| Work item assigned to an approver | Pending approval |
| Approved (final) | Approved, and the customer's limit is updated |
| Rejected | Rejected |
| Request change | Draft |
| Recalled or canceled | Canceled |

**Editing rules:**
- A request can be edited and deleted only in Draft.
- In Submitted or Pending approval every field is read-only (AC-02).
- Approved, Rejected and Canceled requests are read-only and cannot be deleted.

**Submit validation** (AC-01) shows a clear message and blocks the submit when:
- the justification is blank;
- the requested limit equals the current limit;
- the customer is missing;
- the requested limit is negative;
- another open request exists for the customer.

### 3.3 Workflow
Workflow type *Credit limit change approval* (Accounts receivable workflows). It has one approval element, *Approve credit limit change*, with the standard outcomes Approve, Reject, Request change and Delegate. The client builds the routing in the workflow editor using the field **Approval path** (Decrease / Single-step / Two-step), together with the Net change and Requested new limit if needed. Section 5 gives the recommended configuration. Escalation and time limits are standard settings per step.

### 3.4 Parameters
*Accounts receivable > Setup > Accounts receivable parameters*:
- A new tab, **Credit limit approval**, has two fields: *Net increase threshold* and *New limit threshold*.
- The *Number sequences* tab gets a new reference, *Credit limit request ID*.

### 4.3 Locking Credit limit on the customer

**Privilege entry point:**
- New display menu item `DYLO_CustCreditLimitEdit` (object CustTable form, label "Edit customer credit limit"). It is not placed on any menu; it exists only to carry the permission.
- `DYLO_CustCreditLimitEdit` privilege grants it.
- The check is `hasMenuItemAccess(menuItemDisplayStr(DYLO_CustCreditLimitEdit), MenuItemType::Display)`. It is wrapped in `DYLO_CustCreditLimitAccess::canEditCreditLimit()`, a static method on the processor class (4.5).

**Form data source CoC `DYLO_CustTableFormCustTableDS_Extension`** (`[ExtensionOf(formDataSourceStr(CustTable, CustTable))]`, final):
- Wrap `public int active()`. The CustTable form's CustTable data source declares a public `active()`.
- After `next active()`, run:
  `this.object(fieldNum(CustTable, CreditMax)).allowEdit(custTable.RecId != 0 && DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequestWFProcessor::canEditCreditLimit());`
- This runs after the standard change-proposal enablement in `init`, so the lock wins.

**Table CoC `DYLO_CustTable_Extension`** (`[ExtensionOf(tableStr(CustTable))]`, final):
- Wrap `validateWrite()` (public, not final). After `ret = next validateWrite()`, skip the check when `DYLO_CustCreditLimitUpdateContext::isActive()` is true (the workflow update). Otherwise:
  - if `this.RecId == 0 && this.CreditMax != 0`, fail with the creation message (3.1);
  - if `this.RecId != 0 && this.CreditMax != this.orig().CreditMax && !canEditCreditLimit()`, fail with the change message (3.1);
  - a user with the privilege may change the limit on an existing customer, but not on creation.

**Class `DYLO_CustCreditLimitUpdateContext`** (final, implements `System.IDisposable`):
- a static, thread-scoped flag that is set in `new()` and cleared in `dispose()`;
- `static boolean isActive()`.

It is used as `using (var ctx = new DYLO_CustCreditLimitUpdateContext()) { ... }` around the workflow's customer update.

**Query `DYLO_CustCreditLimitReqWFQuery`:**
- Root data source `DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequest` with all fields, so CustAccount, RequestedCreditMax, NetChange, CurrentCreditMax and ApprovalPath can be used in conditions.

**Form workflow settings:** on form `DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequest`, the design has WorkflowEnabled = Yes, WorkflowDataSource = DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequest and WorkflowType = DYLO_CustCreditLimitReqWFType.

**Submit validation:** in the generated submit manager, before `Workflow::activateFromWorkflowType`, call `DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequestWFProcessor::prepareSubmit(request)`. It:
1. refreshes CurrentCreditMax from CustTable;
2. recalculates NetChange;
3. validates (3.2) and throws `error(...)` with the specific message;
4. sets ApprovalPath (assumption 3) from `CustParameters::find()`;
5. writes the record.

On resubmit after Request change, the same call is made.

**Event mapping** (generated handlers call the processor):

| Handler | Event | Processor call |
|---|---|---|
| Type | started | `setStatus(Submitted)` |
| Type | canceled | `setStatus(Canceled)` |
| Type | completed | `complete(recId, correlationId)`: if status is not Rejected or Canceled, apply the approval (below) |
| Approval | started | `setStatus(PendingApproval)` |
| Approval | completed | no status change; the type's completed event finalises |
| Approval | returned (Reject) | `setStatus(Rejected)` |
| Approval | changeRequested | `setStatus(Draft)` |
| Approval | canceled | `setStatus(Canceled)` |
| Approval | created (work item) | `setStatus(PendingApproval)` |

### 4.5 Business logic class `DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequestWFProcessor` (final)
- `static boolean canEditCreditLimit()`: menu item access check (4.3).
- `static void prepareSubmit(DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequest _request)`: as described in 4.4.
- `static DYLO_CustCreditLimitApprovalPath calcApprovalPath(AmountMST _net, CustCreditMaxMST _new, CustParameters _p)`:
  - `_net < 0` gives Decrease;
  - `_net > _p.DYLO_CreditLimitIncreaseThreshold || _new > _p.DYLO_CreditLimitNewLimitThreshold` gives TwoStep;
  - anything else gives SingleStep.
- `static void setStatus(RefRecId _recId, DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequestStatus _status)`: `ttsbegin`, select forUpdate, set, `update()`, `ttscommit`.
- `static void complete(RefRecId _recId, WorkflowCorrelationId _correlationId)`, in one transaction:
  1. Read the request forUpdate; return if the status is Approved, Rejected or Canceled.
  2. Read `CustTable` forUpdate by CustAccount. If CreditMax ≠ CurrentCreditMax, show `info()` (assumption 4).
  3. Inside `using (new DYLO_CustCreditLimitUpdateContext())`: set `custTable.CreditMax = request.RequestedCreditMax`; if `custTable.validateWrite()` passes, call `custTable.update()`, otherwise throw.
  4. Set ApprovalStatus Approved and ApprovalDate = `DateTimeUtil::getSystemDate(DateTimeUtil::getUserPreferredTimeZone())`.
  5. Set FinalApproverId = the `User` of the latest `WorkflowTrackingTable` record for the correlation id, joined to `WorkflowTrackingStatusTable`, with TrackingContext WorkItem and TrackingType Approval. Fall back to `curUserId()`.
  6. Call `request.update()`.

  A failure rolls back both records and leaves the workflow in error, so the administrator can resume it.
- `static boolean validateSubmit(DYLO_CustCreditLimitRequest _request)`: the checks of 3.2, each with its own label message.

## 5. Setup and data
1. **Accounts receivable parameters > Credit limit approval**, in each legal entity: enter the thresholds in the accounting currency, for example Net increase threshold 25,000 and New limit threshold 100,000.
2. **Accounts receivable parameters > Number sequences:** assign a sequence to *Credit limit request ID*, for example format `CLR-######`, or generate it with the number sequence wizard.
3. **System administration > Users:** assign *DYLO Credit limit requester*, *DYLO AR Team Lead* and *DYLO Finance Director* to the right users.
4. **Accounts receivable > Setup > Accounts receivable workflows > New > Credit limit change approval**. Build the workflow in the editor, for example:
   - Approval element *Approve credit limit change*.
   - **Step 1, AR Team Lead:**
     - assignment by role *DYLO AR Team Lead* (single approver: first response);
     - automatic action: auto-approve when *Approval path = Decrease*;
     - time limit 24 hours, with an escalation path to the person or role you choose.
   - **Step 2, Finance Director:**
     - step condition *Approval path = Two-step*;
     - assignment by role *DYLO Finance Director*;
     - time limit 48 hours, with an escalation path.
   - Activate the workflow version.

   The approval path value already reflects the per-legal-entity thresholds, so the conditions need no amounts.
5. Check that *Customer approvals* (customer change proposals) is not enabled for Credit limit, and review who may post **Credit management > Credit limit adjustments** (assumption 9).
