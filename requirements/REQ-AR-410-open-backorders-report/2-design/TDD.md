# TDD — REQ-AR-410 Open Customer Backorders & Fulfillment Risk Summary Report

## 1. Summary

**What you asked for and what this design delivers**

| Feature named in the requirement | Delivered by |
|---|---|
| New report "Open Customer Backorders & Fulfillment Risk Summary", opened from Accounts receivable > Inquiries and reports > Orders | Built (new SSRS report, menu item, menu entry) |
| Dialog parameters: Site / Warehouse, Customer group, Customer account (multi-select), Min days overdue (default 1), Include credit-hold orders (default Yes) | Built |
| Eligible lines: open order, remaining quantity > 0, requested ship date overdue by at least Min days overdue | Built |
| Days late, available physical stock per warehouse, backorder amount in accounting currency | Built |
| Risk badge per line: HIGH (> 7 days), MEDIUM (3–7), LOW (1–2) | Built |
| Four KPI cards: total open backorders (orders), total backorder value, high risk (orders and value), shortage units (uncovered) | Built |
| Lines grouped by customer, with credit limit and account rep in the group header, customer subtotal, grand total | Built |
| Layout as in Figure 1 of the FRD (banner, parameter strip, KPI cards, coloured table header, badges) | Built (one precision design of its own) |
| Clean export to PDF and Excel | Built (fixed-width landscape page, no overlapping items) |

The report is new and built from scratch: a data contract, a UI builder with a multi-select customer lookup, a controller, and a pre-processed data provider that fills two temporary tables. It also has one landscape precision design that follows the FRD mockup. Everything is created in the new extension model **DYLOExtensions** with the prefix **DYLO_**. No standard object is changed except by extension: an extension of the Accounts receivable menu and of one security duty.

Note: standard D365 already has the **Backorder lines** inquiry (Accounts receivable > Inquiries and reports > Orders, menu item `SalesLineBackOrder`). It lists overdue open order lines on screen. It has no stock-shortage figures, risk classes, KPI totals or customer grouping, so it does not replace this report.

## 2. Scope and assumptions

In scope: the report, its dialog, its menu entry, its labels and the security to run it. Out of scope: any change to sales orders, credit management or inventory.

Assumptions (please correct any you disagree with):

1. **Which orders.** Only lines of orders of type *Sales order* are included. Journals, subscriptions, item requirements and returns are left out. A line counts when its status is *Open order* (`SalesStatus::Backorder`), its remaining delivery quantity (`RemainSalesPhysical`) is above 0, and it has a requested ship date (`ShippingDateRequested`). That date must be on or before *today − Min days overdue*. "Today" is the run date in the user's time zone. Lines with no requested ship date are left out.
2. **Min days overdue** must be at least 1. The dialog refuses 0 or a negative value, so every line printed falls into one of the three risk classes.
3. **Site / Warehouse** are two fields in the dialog: Site, and Warehouse (its lookup shows the chosen site's warehouses). Both are optional. They filter on the site and warehouse of the order line. Blank means all.
4. **Customer group** filters on the customer group currently on the customer record. **Customer account** is a list: when nothing is chosen, all customers are included. Grouping and subtotals use the order's customer account.
5. **Credit hold** (as answered: both kinds). When *Include credit-hold orders* is No, the report leaves out two kinds of order: (a) orders that credit management currently holds, where the order is "In credit control"; (b) all orders of customers whose account is blocked (Blocked is anything but "No"). When credit management is not in use, only (b) applies.
6. **Account rep** (as answered: the sales responsible on each sales order). The customer header shows the name of the sales responsible worker of that customer's printed orders. When the orders have different sales responsibles, the header lists the distinct names, separated by ", ". It shows "—" when no order has one.
7. **Credit limit** is the customer's credit limit (`CreditMax`) in accounting currency, printed without decimals.
8. **Days late** = run date − requested ship date, in calendar days.
9. **Risk class**: HIGH when days late > 7, MEDIUM when 3 to 7, LOW when 1 to 2.
10. **Backorder amount** = remaining quantity × net unit price. The net unit price is the line amount ÷ the ordered quantity, so line discounts, discount percent, line charges and the price unit are included. The result is converted from the order currency to the accounting currency at today's exchange rate (default exchange rate type of the ledger). The order's own fixed exchange rate is not used.
11. **Open Qty** shows the remaining quantity in the sales unit, as the FRD says. **Avail. Phys.** is the current available physical stock of the item in the inventory unit. It counts the same product variant (configuration, size, colour, style, version) at the line's site and warehouse, summed over all locations, batches and serials. If one item has several open lines, each line shows the same available stock.
12. **Shortage units (uncovered)** (as answered: per line) = remaining quantity in the **inventory unit** minus available physical stock, never below 0. The KPI card adds these up over all printed lines. The inventory unit is used so both quantities are in the same unit.
13. **KPI cards** (as answered: count orders):
    - "Total open backorders" is the number of distinct sales orders with at least one printed line.
    - "Total backorder value" is the sum of all printed backorder amounts.
    - "High risk" is the number of distinct sales orders with at least one HIGH line, and in brackets the sum of the backorder amounts of the HIGH lines only. The mockup shows this: $10,000 + $35,700 + $18,500 = $64,200.
14. The grand-total Open Qty adds up the quantities of all lines as the mockup shows, even when items have different units.
15. **Amounts** print with the symbol of the company's accounting currency (for example "$") and 2 decimals. Dates print in the user's date format.
16. Customers are sorted by account number, and lines within a customer by sales order then line number.
17. **What in the mockup is not printed:**
    - the sample company, customers, items and amounts (all come from the data);
    - the grey frame and shadow around the page;
    - the caption under the page, "Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations — Custom SSRS Report Layout Design (REQ-AR-410 …)", which is the caption of the mockup.

    The text "Report ID: AR-BO-410" **is** printed, as a fixed label.
18. The report runs in the current company only.
19. Who can run it: the new privilege is added to the standard duty *Inquire into sales order progress* (`SalesOrderProgressInquire`). Roles that hold that duty, such as the sales and customer service roles, get the report. Other roles (for example a credit controller) can be given the privilege `DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskReportGenerate` in Security configuration.

## 3. Functional design

1. The user opens **Accounts receivable > Inquiries and reports > Orders > Open backorder risk summary**.
2. The dialog shows, in this order: Site, Warehouse, Customer group, Customer accounts (tick-list lookup of customers), Min days overdue (default 1, required), Include credit-hold orders (default Yes). The usual Destination / Records to include options of a report dialog stay.
3. On OK the report collects the eligible lines (section 2, points 1–5), works out the figures per line (points 8–12) and prints one landscape page set:
   - **banner** (on every page): company name, report title, "Report ID: AR-BO-410", run date and "Page x of y";
   - **parameter strip**: the values chosen;
   - **four KPI cards**;
   - **one block per customer**: a header with account, name, credit limit, account rep and customer subtotal, then the column header band, then the lines with coloured risk badges;
   - **grand total band**.
4. When no line matches, the banner, parameter strip and KPI cards (all zero) print, followed by the message "No open backorders match the selected criteria."

### 4.5 Data provider — DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskDP

`[SRSReportParameterAttribute(classStr(DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskContract))]`, extends `SrsReportDataProviderPreProcessTempDB`. It has the dataset methods `getLineTmp()` (DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskLineTmp) and `getHeaderTmp()` (DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskHeaderTmp), each with `SRSReportDataSetAttribute`.

`processReport()`:
1. `today = DateTimeUtil::getSystemDate(DateTimeUtil::getUserPreferredTimeZone())`; `cutOff = today − MinDaysOverdue`.
2. Build a `Query`:
   - SalesLine, with ranges SalesStatus = Backorder, RemainSalesPhysical > 0, ShippingDateRequested between 1900-01-02 and cutOff;
   - inner join SalesTable on SalesId, range SalesType = Sales;
   - inner join CustTable on SalesLine.CustAccount = AccountNum, with a range on CustGroup when one is given;
   - inner join InventDim on InventDimId, with ranges on InventSiteId / InventLocationId when given.

   For CustAccountList: when the list is not null and not empty, add one range per value on SalesLine.CustAccount (OR). An empty list means all.
3. When IncludeCreditHold = No:
   - add the range `SalesTable.CredManInCreditControl == NoYes::No`;
   - add the range `CustTable.Blocked == CustVendorBlocked::No`.
4. For each SalesLine / InventDim record:
   - **AvailPhysical**: build a criteria InventDim that holds the line's product dimensions (`InventDim::inventDimProductDimensions` / copy of the product dimension fields), its InventSiteId and InventLocationId, and nothing else. Then `InventAvailabilityProvider::findByItemIdDim(itemId, criteriaDim).parmInventAvailability().availPhysical()`. Cache the result in a Map keyed by itemId + criteria InventDimId, so one item/warehouse is read once.
   - **ShortageQty** = max(0, SalesLine.RemainInventPhysical − AvailPhysical).
   - **Backorder amount**: `netUnit = SalesLine.SalesQty ? SalesLine.LineAmount / SalesLine.SalesQty : 0`; `amountCur = SalesLine.RemainSalesPhysical * netUnit`; `BackorderAmountMST = CurrencyExchangeHelper::amountCur2MST(amountCur, SalesLine.CurrencyCode)` (today's rate by default).
   - **DaysLate** = `today − SalesLine.ShippingDateRequested`. **RiskLevel**: High if DaysLate > 7, Medium if ≥ 3, otherwise Low.
   - **CustName and CustCreditMax**: from CustTable, cached per customer.
   - **ItemName** = `InventTable::find(itemId).itemName(inventDim)`.
   - Keep the order's `WorkerSalesResponsible` in a per-customer Set of names (`HcmWorker::find(...).name()`).
   - Insert the line into the line buffer (`RecordInsertList`).
   - Add to the totals: the Sets of SalesId (all lines, and High lines only), and the sums.
5. After the loop, set AccountRep for each customer with one `update_recordset` per customer, from the joined names in the Set. Cut the text to the field length.
6. Insert the one header row: the parameter texts (InventSite / InventLocation / CustGroup names, "All" label when blank), the totals, CompanyName, CurrencySymbol and RunDate.

The data provider only reads standard data and writes only its own temporary tables.

- The company name comes from CompanyInfo. The credit limit is `CustTable.CreditMax`. The account rep is `SalesTable.WorkerSalesResponsible`.
- The credit-hold field `CredManInCreditControl` is always present when the CreditManagement package is deployed. It is No for every order when credit management is not used.

**Export.** All items sit inside their containers without overlap. The body is exactly 10.00 in wide, so PDF prints on one page width. In Excel, every column maps to one Excel column, because the merged header cells line up with the column boundaries.

## 5. Setup and data

- No parameters or number sequences are needed.
- Security: the report is available to roles that hold the duty *Inquire into sales order progress*. To give it to other roles (for example the credit controller), add the privilege **Generate open backorder risk summary report** (`DYLO_OpenBackorderRiskReportGenerate`) to them in System administration > Security configuration.
- After deployment, the report must be deployed to the report server (standard report deployment of the DYLOExtensions model).
